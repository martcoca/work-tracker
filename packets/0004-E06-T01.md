## Context

`cost-guard` proves no plan introduces idle cost. It runs before anything exists and has
never observed a bill. The organization can therefore prove a negative about the future
and answer nothing about the present.

Read `doctrine/shared/cloud.md` before starting. Its rule — *state what it costs and
whether it is in a free tier* — is the rule this packet turns from a habit into a reading.

**A budget is not a spending control.** It alerts; it does not stop anything. Do not
describe it as protection.

## Goal

Actual spend for the tracker's project is readable on a schedule, and a threshold alerts
before a surprise rather than after one.

## Boundary

**Create nothing the cost guard would refuse.** Budgets and their alerts are free; a
dedicated metrics database is not. If observation appears to need a paid resource, stop —
that is a finding.

**Do not read or store a billing account id in a tracked file.** It is an account
identifier and `doctrine/shared/security.md` forbids committing it. It belongs in
`config/local/` like every other real coordinate.

**Do not touch the AWS side.** The authority exports live in another account under another
initiative; one project first.

## Done when

- Actual spend for the project is retrieved and reported, refreshed on a schedule rather
  than on demand, and the report states the period it covers.
- The report sits **next to the guard's plan-time claim**, so "no idle cost was planned"
  and "this is what it actually cost" are read together rather than in different places.
- A budget with a threshold exists, and the evidence shows what it alerts on and to whom.
- **The alert is shown firing**, against a deliberately low threshold if necessary. A
  budget nobody has seen alert is a configuration, not a control.
- The evidence states plainly what is *not* covered: Actions minutes and model capacity
  are T02, and the largest cost of this organization is the second of those.
