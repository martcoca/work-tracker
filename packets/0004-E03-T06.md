## Context

The Product Specification says *"The chief-of-staff creates packets in the app, and the app
writes them."* Every authoring route — `draft-create`, `draft-update`, `draft-issue`,
`supersession-draft-create` — is gated on `identity.Principal`, the human Identity Platform
verifier, so only a signed-in person can. The Founder, who is the only signed-in person, is
specified as read-and-comment and has said plainly they will never author packets.

[ADR-0058](../../../docs/decisions/0058-the-chief-of-staff-authors-through-the-product.md)
resolves it: authoring becomes a machine operation with its own scopes.
`0000-E05-T06` adds `packet:author`, `packet:issue` and `packet:supersede` to the vocabulary.
This packet is the consuming half.

`0004-E03-T05` already resolves a credential to the workload it acts as, and that workload is
what a grant names. Everything needed to authorize a machine author exists except the check
itself.

## Goal

The chief-of-staff creates, issues and supersedes packets in the app with a credential, and
no person has to be signed in for it to happen.

## Boundary

**Do not remove the human path.** A signed-in human keeps every route they have today. This
adds an acceptable identity; it does not replace one.

**Do not invent scopes.** Use exactly the three `0000-E05-T06` publishes. A scope this
product recognizes and `0000` does not is the drift the conformance vectors exist to prevent.

**Deny by default, per route.** `packet:author` does not permit issuing. `packet:issue` does
not permit superseding. A credential holding one and presented to another is refused, and the
refusal says which scope was missing.

**Nobody edits a packet body** — not a human, not the chief-of-staff, not a session. That
route does not exist and this packet does not add it.

**Do not migrate `packets/`.** That is `0004-E04-T02` and it is gated elsewhere.

## Done when

- Each authoring route accepts **either** a signed-in human **or** an agent credential whose
  workload holds the matching scope in the published grant export.
- **The scope-to-route mapping is exact and tested**: author covers draft create and update,
  issue covers issue, supersede covers supersession. Presenting a credential with the wrong
  one is refused distinguishably, naming the scope required.
- A credential whose workload holds **no** live grant is authenticated and refused, as
  `0004-E03-T05` already requires.
- **An authored packet records who authored it** — the workload, not a person — and that
  attribution appears in the packet's history and survives into the export.
- The human path is unchanged, proven by the existing human-surface tests still passing
  untouched.
- Every rule has a test that fails when the rule is removed. Show the failures.
