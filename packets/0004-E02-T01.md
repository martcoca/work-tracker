## Context

E01 made packets real and readable. Nobody can look at them.

This packet builds the surface the Founder actually asked for: **sign in, select an
initiative, see every packet in it.** It is read-only; authoring is T02.

**Human identity here is Identity Platform**, and it is deliberately *not* shared with
[0000](../../0000-identity-and-tenancy/technical-spec.md). ADR-0046 rejected SSO on cost — the
federated tiers collapse to 50 MAU — so 0000 signs a member in with Cognito and this product
signs a human in with Identity Platform. **The tenant is the only shared fact**, and it comes
from 0000's published directory.

A human's token carries a tenant claim. That claim is checked against the directory, not
trusted.

## Goal

A signed-in human navigates initiative → epic → packet and reads status, body, history and
comments — for their tenant and no other.

## Boundary

**Read-only.** No authoring, no packet creation, no status change, no comment posting. T02
owns writes.

**No apply of cloud identity.** Author the Identity Platform configuration and stop at a clean
plan. Applying it is the Founder's.

No session API. No tenant administration.

## Done when

- A human signs in through Identity Platform, and their **tenant claim is checked against
  0000's published directory** rather than trusted.
- **An unknown tenant is refused; a `retired` tenant is refused differently.**
- Navigation reaches initiative → epic → packet. A packet view shows its frozen body, its
  current status, its full history and its comments.
- **What is waiting is visible**: packets blocked, and packets nobody has taken.
- **Tenant isolation holds in the render path**: requesting tenant B's packets as a member of
  tenant A is refused. Prove it with a test.
- **No route accepts a mutating method**, asserted mechanically against the built route list —
  not by inspection and not by a comment.
- **The product renders with 0000 unavailable**, from the last directory export it holds, and
  says how old that export is.
- WCAG 2.2 AA, checked with a tool. Say which and what it reported.
- `tofu plan` is clean and the cost guard passes it. Idle cost zero.
